I think that it is fascinating that Harriet, the giant tortoise captured by
Charles Darwin in the Galapogos Islands, is still alive. Unfortunately,
Harriet currently lives The Crocodile Hunter's Austrailia Zoo, so there is
virtually no chance of me ever getting to see her. Reading just a little about
[Harriet's
story](http://www.whatsontheplanet.com/wow/ptnr/rci/page.jsp?fx=event&event_id=14769)
instilled in me a sense of awe over the scope of her lifetime. 175 years is a
long time.
