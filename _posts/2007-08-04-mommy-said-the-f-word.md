Well, tonight was the first night that I was in charge of giving Emily her
bath. All said, I do not think that it went too badly for my second time to
ever be involved in giving the baby a bath. She was good throughout the whole
thing and even seemed to be fairly happy through most of it. After I picked
her up and gave her to Ashley to dry, Ashley let the F-Word slip. You know,
the most horrible of words that you can say around a new baby. That is right,
she said _fuss_! Of course, it bothered Emily so much that she decided to do
just that. She fussed. And fussed. And fussed. Finally, we got her dry and
clothed and warm and she seemed to calm down. Ashley is rocking her before bed
right now. Emily has had a big day. She went to a work cookout with Ashley and
I. She got to meet most of my coworkers, their spouses, and kids. She even got
toted around by some of them. After all that excitement, we have had a bit of
a hard time getting her to calm down tonight, but she is just so tired that I
think she will sleep for a while, even though Mommy said the F-Word :)
