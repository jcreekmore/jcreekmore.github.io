Well, Emily has completed her first week at daycare. Ashley and I are slowly
starting to get things together. Every couple of days, we take [a few more
pictures](http://thecreekmores.org/photos/album/first-week-at-daycare/), with
bursts on the weekends. The mornings are kind of hectic. We get up around 6am
so that Ashley can get ready for work and I can get the baby fed and dressed.
Finally, around 7am, I get to eat breakfast, shower, and get dressed. Then, I
take back that baby for about 15 minutes while Ashley finishes getting ready.
We finally walk out the door about 8am. I think we are going to try something
different next week so that we can all get out the door a little earlier.
Since Ashley has dropped back to 30 hours a week for a while, she picks the
baby up around 3:30pm or 4pm. When I get home around 5:30pm, it is time to
prepare dinner for us and the baby. Yes, she wants to eat at the same time
that we do, so we wind up eating in shifts at the moment. She then plays for a
little while before taking a brief, early evening nap before bath-time at 8pm.
After I give her a bath, Ashley takes over rocking and giving one last bottle
before bed. At this point, I am downstairs cleaning bottles, making up bottles
for the next day, cleaning up after dinner, and making lunches. It takes each
of us about 30 minutes to finish our tasks, so Little Bit usually goes down
between 9pm and 9:30pm. Normally, she is down for the night until around 6am
the next day.
