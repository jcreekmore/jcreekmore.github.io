Being an introvert, I have always need time to myself to rest and recharge my
batteries. Since Emily began talking, I have come to appreciate complete and
utter silence during my me-time. Before, I might watch TV, play a video game,
or read or mess around on the computer while listening to music. Now that
Emily talks non-stop from waking until sleep, I know spend my time to myself
reading in silence. The few mornings that Ashley lets me "sleep in" while she
gets Emily up, I am just as likely to lay in bed for a half hour, just
reveling in the quiet. I have come to truly believe that silence is golden.
