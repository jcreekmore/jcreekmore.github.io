While killing time on the site, I decided to create a parent category for the
two dog categories, [Snobbers](http://thecreekmores.org/category/canine-
commandos/snobbers/) and [The Gutless
Wonder](http://thecreekmores.org/category/canine-commandos/the-gutless-
wonder). My choice was [Canine
Commandos](http://thecreekmores.org/category/canine-commandos/). While
checking the spelling, I realized just how appropriate my choice of
[commandos](http://dictionary.reference.com/search?q=commandos) was: "A small
fighting force specially trained for making quick destructive raids against
enemy-held areas." Some days that description seems to fit our dogs exactly.
