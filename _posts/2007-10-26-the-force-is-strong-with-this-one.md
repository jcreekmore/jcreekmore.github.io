So, when Ashley broached the idea of a Halloween costume for Emily, I was a
little hesitant. My thoughts were, "She is so young, she will not remember
anything we put her in, so why bother?" My next thought was, "She is so young,
she will not remember anything we put her in. This is the perfect opportunity
to dress her in something embarrassingly cute!" So, without further ado, I
present [our daughter,
Yoda](http://thecreekmores.org/photos/album/halloween-2007/). I mean, really,
how often do you get to dress your daughter up as an 800 year old little green
man?
