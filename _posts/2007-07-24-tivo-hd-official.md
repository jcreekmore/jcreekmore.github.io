While most of our readers will not care, the [TiVo HD is now
official](http://www.tivo.com/whatistivo/tivohd/index.html). From a storage
standpoint, the TiVo HD will not store any more hours of HD programming than
the Knology DVR that I currently have. However, I would be gaining the nice,
shiny TiVo software that I fondly remember in my pre-HD days. While TiVo has
had an HD capable DVR (the Series 3) for about a year now, the Series 3 rang
in at around $800. The new TiVo HD will retail for a mere $299 -- a much more
affordable price for those of us with a new baby. Now, I just need to confirm
that Knology will provide CableCARDs and I can finally ditch their DVR for
TiVo goodness once again.
