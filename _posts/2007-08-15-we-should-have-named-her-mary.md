Last week, I mentioned that Emily [started her first week at
daycare](http://thecreekmores.org/2007/08/11/first-week-at-daycare/). Although
I did not yet know it, she came home at the end of that first week with her
first cold. It is now half-way through her second week of day care and I have
come down with a cold. Now, I knew that having a kid would mean that you get
sick more often. I just did not realize that it would happen this quickly.
