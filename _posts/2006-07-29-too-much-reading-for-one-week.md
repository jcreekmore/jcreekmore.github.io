Well, I did not think that it would happen, but I have hit my limit for books
in the past week. Early in the week, I was working my way through two books
for work. I read the majority of [VHDL : Programming By
Example](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?tag=manalangcom-20%26link_code=xm2%26camp=2025%26creative=165953%26path=http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html%253fASIN=0071400702%2526tag=manalangcom-20%2526lcode=xm2%2526cID=2025%2526ccmID=165953%2526location=/o/ASIN/0071400702%25253FSubscriptionId=0EMV44A9A5YT1RVDGZ82
"View product details at Amazon") and managed to make it half way through
[Rapid
Development](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?tag=manalangcom-20%26link_code=xm2%26camp=2025%26creative=165953%26path=http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html%253fASIN=1556159005%2526tag=manalangcom-20%2526lcode=xm2%2526cID=2025%2526ccmID=165953%2526location=/o/ASIN/1556159005%25253FSubscriptionId=0EMV44A9A5YT1RVDGZ82
"View product details at Amazon"). I read the VHDL book as a refresher for the
project I am currently involved with at work. I am reading the book on Rapid
Development to gain insight on software project management and to work towards
my long-term career goals. Amazingly, I also managed to finish two fiction
books this week. During the first part of the week, I read [The Soulforge
(Dragonlance: The Raistlin Chronicles, Book
1)](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?tag=manalangcom-20%26link_code=xm2%26camp=2025%26creative=165953%26path=http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html%253fASIN=0786913142%2526tag=manalangcom-20%2526lcode=xm2%2526cID=2025%2526ccmID=165953%2526location=/o/ASIN/0786913142%25253FSubscriptionId=0EMV44A9A5YT1RVDGZ82
"View product details at Amazon"), which was a paperback I picked up at a used
bookstore several months ago. It was so entertaining that I read it through at
a break-neck pace just so that I could find out what would happen next to
Raistlin. During the latter half of the week, I read [Phantom: Chainfire
Trilogy, Part 2 (Sword of Truth, Book
10)](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?tag=manalangcom-20%26link_code=xm2%26camp=2025%26creative=165953%26path=http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html%253fASIN=0765305240%2526tag=manalangcom-20%2526lcode=xm2%2526cID=2025%2526ccmID=165953%2526location=/o/ASIN/0765305240%25253FSubscriptionId=0EMV44A9A5YT1RVDGZ82
"View product details at Amazon"). I have been waiting on this book to come
out for the last year and a half, so I am sad that I finished it in two days.
Now, there is only one book left in the series and it is probably another year
or two away. Given the size of the books, I estimated that I read somewhere
around 1500 pages this week, give or take a few hundred. Now, I am trying to
rest for the remainder of the weekend before I try to finish Rapid Development
during the next week.
