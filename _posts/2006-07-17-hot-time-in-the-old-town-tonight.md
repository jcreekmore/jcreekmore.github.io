Alabama gets rather warm during the summer months. The current forecast for
today is for the high to reach around 101 degrees Fahrenheit. As if that were
not bad enough, the compressor on one of our two heat-pumps, the upstairs
unit, has gone out and the unit needs to be replaced. In addition, the duct-
work for both the upstairs and downstairs units are falling apart. We are
currently in the process of getting estimates for the replacement and repair
work. Luckily, the downstairs unit works, although we are pretty sure that we
are trying to cool the attic in the process. Thus, we are only able to run the
downstairs unit at about 40% efficiency at the moment. Between that and fans,
the downstairs is relatively livable. We are also avoiding going upstairs
whenever possible, but it is not too bad in the early morning hours.
