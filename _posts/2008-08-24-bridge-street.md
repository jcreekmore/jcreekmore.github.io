During the weekend, we try to get out of the house for a little while each
day. Emily does much better if she has an outing, so we try to come up with
somewhere to go. Sometimes it is tough to figure out something to do that is
inexpensive and will keep Emily's attention for more than 2 seconds. Yesterday
evening, we decided to head to Bridge Street and just let her [wander
around](http://thecreekmores.org/photos/album/emily-at-bridge-street/). She
seemed to enjoy playing in the water the most, but she really liked just
getting to walk with her monkey on her back (its tail is a leash). When she
got tired, we had the stroller for her, but she liked riding around on Daddy's
shoulders more than the stroller.
