It all started so innocently. Driving back to the house, I decide to stop by
the library to see if the book I placed on hold has come in yet. I was just
there yesterday, picking up two other books that I had on hold--is _this_ one
here yet? Alas, it is not. No matter; I can do a little research while I am
here. Hmm, Nolo's book on [estate
planning](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1413312012?ie=UTF8&tag=jonathcreekm-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=1413312012)![](http://www.assoc-
amazon.com/e/ir?t=jonathcreekm-20&l=as2&o=1&a=1413312012) appears to be that
for which I am looking. Satisfied with my find, I head back to the front to
check out and notice the [Great Courses](http://www.teach12.com/) on CD and
pick up the [great debate on the American
Constitution](http://www.teach12.com/tgc/courses/course_detail.aspx?cid=4878).
Now, I am completely distracted and wander the shelves, idly picking up books
that catch my eye until, 30 minutes later, I find myself walking out of the
library with a total of six books and twelve half-hour lectures on CD. Now, I
am sitting at home, happy as a clam, surrounded by a mass of books from the
library and that I received for Christmas. But, as you can see, I have a small
problem. What should I read first?
