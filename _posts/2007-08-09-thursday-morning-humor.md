[A recent
post](http://scienceblogs.com/goodmath/2007/08/bad_math_education_math_does_n.php)
on Good Math, Bad Math caught my attention this morning. The article talk
about mathematics being independent of a deity; mathematics is based on
abstract reasoning so it will be consistent whether the universe was created
by a benevolent deity or whether it just is. The article is in response to a
geometry class description at a Baptist high school. However, a [comment by
chaos_engineer](http://scienceblogs.com/goodmath/2007/08/bad_math_education_math_does_n.php#comment-527992)
just made me laugh out loud.

> Of course. Christians would be taught real geometry, with the Euclidean
> version of the parallel postulate. People who practice flawed religions
> would naturally be more comfortable studying flawed, non-Euclidean
> geometries. Jews could learn Riemann's version of the parallel postulate,
> Muslims could learn Lobachevksy's, and atheists could learn H. P.
> Lovecraft's.

OK, so maybe it is just funny if you are an amateur mathematician and a fan of
H. P. Lovecraft. However, I am both :)
