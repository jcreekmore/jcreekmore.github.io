Time is relative, but in other ways than the way Einstein necessarily meant.
For instance, I met with my Monday night group on Sunday this week, my Tuesday
night group on Monday, and now today (Tuesday) has felt like Wednesday all day
long. To top it all off, I will probably call my parents tonight and, since I
normally talk to them on Sunday, that means tomorrow will feel like Monday all
over again.
