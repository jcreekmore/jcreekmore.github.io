Deep in the night, a mournful cry is lifted to the heavens. Is it a lonesome
coyote, searching for a mate? Or perhaps a solitary wolf, finding the scent of
a quickly fleeing rabbit and gearing up for a mighty chase? Or could it be a
majestic hound detecting a fox nearby and alerting everyone that the hunt has
begun? Who can know what goes on in the mind of a slumbering pup to cause him
to lift his mournful cry in the wee hours of the morning...
