What do I spend all of my time doing? Well, lately it has been trying to
launch a website for the [new D&D; campaign](http://rpg.thecreekmores.org)
that I am running for my gaming group. This has been fun, mainly because I
have been nerdily combining D&D; with a Drupal-based website. I have had a
mailing list set up for the local gaming group for years now; definitely
longer than Emily has been around, probably going on three years or so. This
takes the nerdiness to a whole new level with accounts for each character
(yes, even some of my NPCs will have accounts), forums for campaign notes and
in-game discussions, and support for each character to have a diary/blog of
their very own. The amount of time I have spent learning a little about Drupal
and setting this site up still does not touch the amount of time I spend every
week preparing for the game. However, I think it will be a good resource for
the players as the game progresses.
